For Educators

School Group Visits

We work with primary and secondary schools to create curriculum-linked experiences that bring STEM subjects to life. Our education team will help you plan a visit tailored to your group's age and learning objectives.

  • Curriculum-linked workshop programmes (KS1–KS4)
  • Dedicated group entry from just £8 per pupil
  • Free entry for up to 1 adult per 5 pupils
  • Teacher resource packs for pre-visit preparation
  • STEM ambassador Q&A sessions on request

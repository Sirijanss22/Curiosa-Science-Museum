Getting Here

How to Find Us

🗺️

🚇 Underground

Barbican (5 min) or Farringdon (7 min). Circle, H&C, Metropolitan, and Elizabeth lines.

🚌 By Bus

Routes 56, 153, 243 and 63 stop on Aldersgate Street — 3 min walk. Frequent service.

🚗 By Car

ULEZ zone. Smithfield car park 10 min walk. Free secure cycle parking at entrance.

Accessibility

We are committed to making science accessible to everyone. Contact us in advance with any specific requirements.

♿

Wheelchair Access

All floors accessible via lifts and ramps. Accessible toilets on every level. Free wheelchair hire from reception.

👁️

Visual Impairment

Audio guides and Braille signage throughout. Guide dogs welcome. Tactile models in key zones.

👂

Hearing Impairment

Hearing loops in all theatres. BSL-interpreted planetarium shows monthly. Written summaries available.

🧡

Sensory & Neurodiversity

Quiet hours Tue & Thu 10:00–12:00. Sensory kits on loan. Dedicated quiet room on Level 1.

Common Questions

Can I bring food and drink?

Outside food and non-alcoholic beverages are welcome in our picnic areas and garden. Outside food cannot be consumed in exhibition zones.

Is there somewhere to store buggies?

Yes — free left-luggage lockers and buggy storage near the main entrance. Buggies can also be taken into most exhibition areas.

Are dogs allowed?

Only assistance dogs are permitted inside. Our outdoor garden is dog-friendly, with water available at the entrance.

Is the museum suitable for under-5s?

Absolutely! Our Discovery Tots area is designed for under-5s with soft play and sensory stations. Under-3s enter free.

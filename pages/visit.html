Getting Here

How to Find Us

🗺️
🚇

By Underground

Barbican station (Circle, Hammersmith & City, Metropolitan lines) — 5 min walk. Farringdon station (Elizabeth, Circle, H&C, Thameslink) — 7 min walk.

🚌

By Bus

Routes 56, 153, 243, and 63 all stop on Aldersgate Street, a 3-minute walk from the museum entrance. Routes run frequently.

🚗

By Car

We are in the London ULEZ zone. Limited parking available at Smithfield car park (10 min walk). Cycle parking is free and secure at the museum entrance.

Inclusive museum

Accessibility

We are committed to making science accessible to everyone. If you have specific accessibility requirements, please contact us in advance so we can prepare.

♿

Wheelchair Access

All floors are fully wheelchair accessible via lifts and ramps. Accessible toilets on every level. Wheelchair hire available free of charge from reception.

👁️

Visual Impairment

Audio guides available for all major exhibitions. Braille signage throughout. Guide dogs warmly welcomed. Tactile models in our key zones.

👂

Hearing Impairment

Hearing loop systems in all theatre and demonstration areas. BSL-interpreted planetarium shows run twice monthly. Written programme summaries always available.

🧡

Sensory & Neurodiversity

Quiet hours every Tuesday and Thursday morning (10:00–12:00) with reduced lighting and sound levels. Sensory kits available on loan from reception. Dedicated quiet room on Level 1.

FAQs

Common Questions

Can I bring food and drink?

Outside food and non-alcoholic beverages are welcome in our picnic areas on Level 1 and in the garden. Our café serves hot meals, snacks, and drinks. Outside food cannot be consumed in the exhibition zones.

Is there somewhere to store bags and buggies?

Yes! We have free left-luggage lockers and buggy storage near the main entrance. Buggies can also be taken into most exhibition areas.

Are dogs allowed?

Only assistance dogs are permitted inside the museum. However, our outdoor garden area is dog-friendly and there is water available at the entrance for your pet.

What is your photography policy?

Personal photography and filming is welcome throughout the museum for non-commercial use. Please be considerate of other visitors. Commercial photography requires prior permission from our press office.

Do you offer cloakroom facilities?

Yes, a manned cloakroom is available daily near the main entrance for a £2 charge per item. Lockers are also available free of charge for smaller items.

Is the museum suitable for under-5s?

Absolutely! Our Discovery Tots area on Level 1 is specifically designed for children under 5, with soft play, sensory play, and interactive story stations. Under-3s enter free.

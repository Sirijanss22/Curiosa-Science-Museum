Explore All Zones

Space · Level 2

Cosmology: The Big Picture

Journey from the Big Bang to the edge of the observable universe. A 1:10 billion scale model of the solar system, AR nebula viewers, and a genuine lunar meteorite you can actually touch.

  • ★ Touch a real lunar meteorite
  • ★ AR telescope — live images from space
  • ★ Scale model solar system walk
  • ★ Daily planetarium shows
Book planetarium show 🕒 Allow 60–90 min
🦕
Biology · Level 1

Evolution: Life Through Time

Walk a timeline of 3.5 billion years. Life-size animatronic dinosaurs, fossil excavation, and real DNA extraction experiments.

  • ★ Life-size animatronic T. rex
  • ★ Fossil excavation pit (all ages)
  • ★ DNA extraction lab
  • ★ Human family tree display
🕒 Allow 45–75 min
🤖
Technology · Level 3

Robotics & AI Lab

Programme real robots with block coding, race them on our track, and explore how AI learns. Our most popular zone — book workshops in advance.

  • ★ 20+ programmable robots
  • ★ Block-coding station for beginners
  • ★ AI image recognition challenge
  • ★ 3D printing demo every hour
Book workshop 🕒 Allow 60–90 min

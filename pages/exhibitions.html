What's inside

Explore All Zones

Each zone is packed with hands-on activities, interactive displays, and live demonstrations.

Space · Level 2

Cosmology: The Big Picture

Journey from the Big Bang to the edge of the observable universe. Features a 1:10 billion scale model of the solar system, augmented reality nebula viewers, and a genuine lunar meteorite you can actually touch.

  • ★ Touch a real lunar meteorite
  • ★ AR telescope — view live images from space
  • ★ Scale model solar system walk
  • ★ Daily planetarium shows
Book planetarium show 🕒 Allow 60–90 min
Biology · Level 1

Evolution: Life Through Time

Walk a timeline stretching 3.5 billion years. Come face-to-face with animatronic dinosaurs, examine fossil casts, and discover how DNA connects all living things.

  • ★ Life-size animatronic T. rex
  • ★ Fossil excavation pit (all ages)
  • ★ DNA extraction lab
  • ★ Human family tree interactive display
🕒 Allow 45–75 min
Technology · Level 3

Robotics & AI Lab

Programme robots using block coding, race them on our track, and explore how artificial intelligence learns. Daily workshops every weekend and school holiday.

  • ★ 20+ programmable robots to control
  • ★ Block-coding station for beginners
  • ★ AI image recognition challenge
  • ★ 3D printing demonstration every hour
Book workshop 🕒 Allow 60–90 min
